Lombok generates the boilerplate during compilation.

Common Lombok annotations

@Getter-	Getters
@Setter-	Setters
@ToString-	toString()
@EqualsAndHashCode-	equals() and hashCode()
@NoArgsConstructor-	No-argument constructor
@AllArgsConstructor-	Constructor with all fields
@RequiredArgsConstructor-	Constructor for required fields (which is marked the final or not null generally)
@Data-	Getter + Setter + ToString + Equals/HashCode + required constructor
@Builder-	Builder pattern(prevent telescopic constructor hell)
@Slf4j-	Creates a logger
